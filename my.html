FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sprachwerkAI.

How does sprachwerkAI pick up and answer calls?

sprachwerkAI integrates with your existing phone system. When a call comes in, our AI answers it naturally within 500ms, understands the caller's intent, and handles the conversation — just like a human receptionist would.

What languages does sprachwerkAI support?

sprachwerkAI supports over 50 languages including English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese, Mandarin, Arabic, and many more, with native-quality pronunciation and cultural awareness.

Is sprachwerkAI available 24/7?

Yes. sprachwerkAI operates around the clock, every day of the year. Never miss a call again — whether it's 3am, a weekend, or a public holiday.

Can sprachwerkAI transfer calls to a human?

Absolutely. You can set rules for when the AI should transfer to a human agent, take a message, schedule an appointment, or escalate urgent matters. The human receives full context from the conversation.

How long does setup take?

Most businesses are live within 15 minutes. Simply forward your number, tell the AI about your business, and you're ready to go. No technical expertise required.
